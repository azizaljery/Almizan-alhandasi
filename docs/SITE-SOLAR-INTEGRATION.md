# Site solar context and rendering

`siteSolar` is optional version-1 project metadata. It stores an explicit location source, latitude/longitude when manually supplied, an ISO timestamp string with its supplied offset, a north angle clockwise from plan +Y, and the user's north confirmation. There is no default location, current-time fallback, geolocation request, inferred timezone, or provider call.

Project metadata acceptance is distinct from calculation acceptance. Bounded unfinished/invalid timestamp strings and finite numeric drafts may be saved and restored, including in history. The strict `calculateSiteSolar` validator must accept all required values before the adapter returns `approximate-site`; invalid or missing data produce `not-calculated`, no solar light and no solar shadow. This keeps an invalid historical solar draft from blocking a valid geometry download. It does not relax the mathematical API's validation.

The optional survey source uses the first WGS84 `[longitude, latitude]` vertex of the rendered model's `modelLandSurvey`, never `mapView.center` or draft `landSurvey`. It requires explicit model association, a linked rectangle, re-derived binding dimensions that match both the saved binding and the model plot, and an independent manual north confirmation tied to the survey fingerprint. It is a boundary point, not a parcel centroid or a claim of survey accuracy. Changed associated survey fingerprints invalidate confirmation. Alternative selection preserves the associated survey; generation history/undo and project/archive restoration carry their respective solar contexts.

Legacy projects lacking an explicit `modelLandSurvey` field retain an unverified association flag, even when the general project decoder preserves its historical draft-survey fallback. Re-saving does not promote that flag. Malformed association flag values fail closed. A manually entered location remains available without inferred survey provenance.

The site solver's rotated plan/world vectors are authoritative for both 2D shadow displacement and 3D solar direction, including an undefined azimuth at zenith. The compass indicates model +Y until confirmed site north is available; its true-N arrow then rotates by the confirmed angle, even when shadows are hidden or the sun is below the horizon. The geometry is never rotated or regenerated by solar settings.

Shadows are approximate flat-ground projections of a 3.2m representative mass. This is not a daylight, illuminance, energy, atmospheric-refraction, terrain or neighbouring-obstruction analysis. The separately labelled studio lighting remains a nonphysical preview aid and does not substitute for site sunlight.
